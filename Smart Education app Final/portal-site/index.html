About the institute

GSSS Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women, Mysuru, was started by GSSS(R) with the vision of its founder, the late Prof. B. S. Pandit, to provide quality technical education to women. It was Karnataka's first engineering college exclusively for women.

This portal is a student-built prototype for the ECE department, made for HackNext'26 (PS06, Smart Education).

What the portal does

Four features, one login for each role.

Attendance alerts

Students see exactly how many classes they must attend to stay eligible for exams, not just a percentage.

Anonymous doubts

Students flag the topic they didn't understand. Faculty see the count per topic, never the name.

Private counseling

A direct, private line between a student and her counselor, with replies in the same place.

NAAC/NBA report

Faculty records, re-teaching and attendance monitoring become a printable evidence report.

Install it as an app

No download file needed. Use the blue Install app button at the bottom-right of this page, or follow the steps for your device. The app gets its own icon, opens in its own window and works offline.

Windows / Mac / Linux

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click Install app (bottom-right), then Install.
  3. Find the icon on your desktop or Start menu.

Download for Windows (.zip)

Or: download, unzip, double-click Install-Windows.bat (works without internet). If Windows warns, click More info then Run anyway.

Android phone

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app, then Install.
  3. The icon appears on your home screen.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
